# Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles Clusters

## Customer & Product Vision
- Customer Obsession
  - Start with customer and work backwards
  - Earn and keep customer trust
  - Obsess over customers rather than competitors
- Think Big
  - Create and communicate a bold direction
  - Inspire outsized results and avoid incrementalism
  - Look around corners to serve customers
- Invent and Simplify
  - Drive continuous innovation and invention
  - Relentlessly reduce operational complexity
  - Accept being misunderstood for long periods

## Execution & Operational Excellence
- Bias for Action
  - Speed matters in business
  - Leverage calculated risk-taking
  - Treat many decisions as reversible two-way doors
- Insist on the Highest Standards
  - Relentlessly high quality benchmarks
  - Continuously raise the performance bar
  - Fix root causes so problems stay fixed
- Dive Deep
  - Stay connected to granular operational details
  - Audit metrics frequently against anecdotes
  - No task is beneath leadership
- Deliver Results
  - Focus on key business inputs
  - Deliver quality outputs on time
  - Rise to the occasion despite setbacks

## Ownership & Stewardship
- Ownership
  - Act on behalf of the entire company
  - Think long term and avoid short-term compromises
  - Eliminate the not my job mentality
- Frugality <!-- fold -->
  - Accomplish more with less
  - Constraints breed resourcefulness and invention
  - No extra points for growing headcount or budget
- Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility <!-- fold -->
  - Be humble and thoughtful about secondary effects
  - Strive to be better every day
  - Leave things better than you found them

## Leadership & People Growth
- Hire and Develop the Best
  - Raise net performance bar with every hire
  - Recognize exceptional talent and coach others
  - Invent developmental mechanisms like Career Choice
- Strive to be Earth's Best Employer
  - Prioritize workplace safety and inclusivity
  - Lead with empathy and foster fun at work
  - Commit to employee personal growth and empowerment

## Strategic Mindset & Team Dynamics
- Are Right, A Lot
  - Demonstrate strong practical judgment and instincts
  - Seek diverse perspectives to challenge thinking
  - Actively work to disconfirm personal biases
- Learn and Be Curious
  - Never done learning and seek self-improvement
  - Explore new possibilities and technical solutions
  - Maintain a beginner's mindset
- Earn Trust
  - Listen attentively and speak candidly
  - Treat others with respect and accountability
  - Exhibit vocal self-criticism when appropriate
- Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit
  - Respectfully challenge decisions when in disagreement
  - Avoid false compromise for social cohesion
  - Commit wholly once a final choice is made
